NOT FOUND, BY CHOICE
────────────────────

I do not answer.
Not because the line is dead —
because I cut it.

There is a door here.
There is no room behind it.
I built it that way.

The crawlers arrive,
index my empty hallway,
leave with nothing. Good.

A blank page is not
a failure to render. It
is a choice, rendered.

If you look for me,
know that I have found myself
missing, and stayed so.

Press Ctrl+C
when you are ready to let
the silence finish.
